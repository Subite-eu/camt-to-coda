# Worldwide source sweep — 11 October 2026

This follow-up adds **40 bank/brand entries**, one authority reference and **15 distinct CAMT XML originals or unchanged payloads**. The resulting catalog has **97 banks/brands, 58 authorities and 61 geography/scope groups**. This is a curated public-source inventory, not a census of ISO 20022 adoption or a guarantee of customer eligibility.

The [canonical inventory](source-inventory.json) pins download length/hash, exact member names, member length/hash when extracting embedded payloads, payload byte ranges and final original length/hash. All 15 new payloads remain external-only. The 22 attributed Bundesbank originals remain the only committed bank XML. Required CI acquires all **97 external** payloads and tests all **119** originals: **107 XSD-valid, 5 invalid as published, 5 missing element namespaces, and 2 wrapped references**.

Bank of Korea's stable download redirects to its official `file-cdn.bok.or.kr` CDN. Only that explicitly reviewed HTTPS origin is permitted, with a bounded redirect chain and final container/payload integrity checks. Expiring signed CDN URLs are not committed.

## Expanded usage and national-reference evidence

New records cover Canada, Mexico, Brazil, China, Taiwan, Singapore and UOB's documented regional service markets; European banks in Switzerland, Liechtenstein, Estonia, Austria, Croatia, Slovakia, Czech Republic, Germany, France, Poland and the United Kingdom; and bank reporting availability in Malawi. BNY's on-demand interbank statements are explicitly distinguished from corporate reporting. Headquarters do not establish service geography, and regional product coverage does not establish identical profiles or entitlement in every market.

The new [Bank of Korea authority reference](country-references.json) documents BOK-Wire+ interbank reporting, not a national corporate CAMT profile. Finance Finland's actual [CAMT053 sample](https://file.finanssiala.fi/maksujenvalitys/FI_camt_053_sample.xml) was downloaded and checked against v02 XSD; its URL, size and hash are recorded on the authority resource. It is not counted among bank-published originals.

Guides, PDF snippets, registration portals, historical/future-effective specifications and downloadable examples retain different resource/access labels. The [machine-readable bank evidence](catalog.json) and generated geography pages contain exact official links and per-source scope notes. Unacquired RAR examples, CAMT054 and guides containing no XML do not inflate original-file counts.

## Belgian CODA investigation

See the separate [CODA publisher corpus and research](../coda-published/README.md). Official Belfius, KBC, ING, Crelan, Argenta, Beobank and Bank Van Breda pages establish CODA service/export availability; BNP Paribas Fortis publishes historical field snippets. None yielded a verified complete anonymous bank-issued CODA original.

The sweep downloaded **65 third-party fixture files**, deduplicated to **29 distinct decoded/newline-normalized contents**. Those developer/vendor sources are separately classified, preserve original bytes and encoding, and have their own required CI tests. A bank BIC or realistic account statement layout does not prove bank authorship. The complete bank-published CODA acquisition gap therefore remains open.
